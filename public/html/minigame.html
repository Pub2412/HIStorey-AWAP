Inspired by Captain EO

Captain EO is a 1986 American 3D science fiction film starring Michael Jackson, directed by Francis Ford Coppola and executive produced by George Lucas. It tells the story of Captain EO and his ragtag crew on a mission to deliver a musical gift to a wicked alien queen. The innovative visual effects and unforgettable original songs cemented it as a beloved classic.
